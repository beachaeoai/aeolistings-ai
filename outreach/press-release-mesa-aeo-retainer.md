# Mesa, Arizona AEO agency launches month-to-month retainer for home service contractors — no annual contract

*AEO Listings LLC brings Answer Engine Optimization to Phoenix-metro contractors and local professionals with a cancel-by-email retainer model that undercuts the industry standard 12-month lock-in.*

**MESA, ARIZONA — [DATE]** — AEO Listings LLC, a Mesa-based Answer Engine Optimization agency serving home service contractors and local professional practices, today formally launched its month-to-month retainer model — becoming one of the few AEO agencies nationally to offer the discipline without an annual contract commitment.

The agency specializes in getting local service businesses recommended by AI chatbots — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overview — when prospects ask for a recommendation in categories like plumbing, HVAC, roofing, electrical, landscaping, painting, and remodeling.

"When someone in Phoenix asks ChatGPT who's a good plumber, the model either names your business or it doesn't. There is no page two of AI answers," said Jake Beach, founder of AEO Listings LLC. "That means the discipline of getting cited by AI is different from traditional SEO — and it's moving fast enough that a twelve-month contract is a bet neither party should be asked to make. If the work isn't earning its keep every month, the client should be able to cancel by email, not talk their way out of a lawyer letter."

### The gap the agency addresses

According to industry research, generative AI answer engines now handle a growing share of the "who should I hire" queries that traditionally went to Google. For home service contractors specifically, buyer-intent prompts like "best HVAC in [city]" or "plumber for a slab leak near me" are increasingly answered by AI chatbots that name zero to three businesses per response, rather than the traditional ten-link results page.

Local businesses that were fine on Google alone two years ago are visibly losing share of top-of-funnel discovery to competitors that are being cited by AI, Beach said — and most don't know it's happening because they're not measuring citation share.

AEO Listings publishes a public citation-audit method that any business can run in about an hour to check its own AI visibility across the five major answer engines. The agency uses the same methodology as the baseline for every client engagement.

### The four-track service stack

Client engagements run across four tracks: **Foundation** (Google Business Profile audit, NAP citation consistency, Schema.org structured data deployment); **Visibility** (service page restructuring for AI retrieval, FAQPage schema, neighborhood-level content); **Authority** (industry-specific directory placements, trade press mentions, off-site corroboration); and **Operations** (monthly citation tracking across 40-60 buyer-intent prompts per client).

Each track has a documented 30/60/90-day calendar of deliverables, so clients see specific work products at defined intervals rather than a monthly invoice against vague retainer language.

Pricing runs from low four figures monthly for single-track Foundation engagements to mid-to-high four figures for the full four-track stack.

### Focused market

The agency is headquartered in Mesa, Arizona with a regional concentration in the East Valley and Phoenix metro — publishing documented market analyses for Mesa, Phoenix, Scottsdale, Tempe, Chandler, and Gilbert. The service area is nationwide; delivery is remote.

AEO Listings works with home service contractors and local professional practices doing roughly $500,000 to $10 million in annual revenue. The agency turns down work outside that band, work with direct competitors of existing clients, and all requests for paid ad management, website redesigns, or generic SEO churn.
