## Why this matters more than more on-site content

The baseline audit identified corroboration as one of two SEVERE gaps for AEO Listings — nine strong blog posts on aeolistings.ai are pointed inward. AI answer engines don't cite content that lives only on your own domain for competitive category prompts; they cite content *about* you that lives on *other* domains. First Page Sage, OGTool, and NoGood dominate the category not because their content is better than ours but because they're named on ten-plus third-party lists each. AEO Listings is currently named on zero. Fixing that ratio is what unlocks the Category 1 prompts.

## Target: 2 placements from 5 pitches within 30 days

Reasonable hit rate for cold outreach in this category is 30-50% of sends resulting in either "yes send materials" or "yes on the next update." Two placements from these five specific targets would put AEO Listings on the citation-frequency map for the highest-value AEO agency prompts on Google AI Overview.

Combined with the press release (one submission produces ~15-25 regional syndications), this batch alone should move the July→August citation delta materially.
