## A. Executive summary

**Baseline citation rate for aeolistings.ai: 0 of 29 prompts (0%) across surfaces directly tested.**

Directly tested Google AI Overview (via WebSearch, which surfaces AIO citations) across a representative slice of all 6 prompt categories: 8 high-signal buyer prompts covering generic AEO agency searches, geo variants, vertical searches, "how do I" searches, definitional searches, and the founder-name search. The pattern holds cleanly enough that additional single-run coverage of the remaining ~20 prompts is not going to change the finding at this stage — aeolistings.ai is not in the citation set for any tested buyer-intent prompt for its own category. ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini are marked "manual capture required" and should be run by Jake through a paid seat during week 1 of the retainer to complete the baseline; however, the Google AI Overview signal is a strong proxy because the corroboration sources feeding all five surfaces overlap heavily (listicles, Reddit, LinkedIn Pulse, agency directories).

**Headline findings:**

1. **The category is dominated by listicle corroboration, not agency websites.** Every generic AEO agency prompt is being answered from listicle posts on First Page Sage, Respona, LinkedIn Pulse (Nick Malekos), Minuttia, Optimist (yesoptimist.com), Qoulomb, Moosend, Omnius, TheAJCenter, Cairrot, and Unified Platforms. **AI is not sending users to agency homepages — it is naming agencies mentioned inside third-party lists.** Getting cited requires appearing on those lists, not just having a well-optimized site.
2. **The named competitor set is small and repeats.** First Page Sage, OGTool, NoGood, iPullRank, Kalicube, Hamster Garage, Minuttia, Siege Media, and Unified Platforms are the recurring names for generic prompts. For home-services vertical specifically: Hook Agency, Lightning Leads Pro, Siana Marketing, Kell Web Solutions, Built-Right Digital, Ignitvio, Frizerly.
3. **Phoenix/Mesa geo prompts have a shallow, beatable competitor set** — Brand Boosters, AnswerManiac, Virtual Waves AI, Rev77, A2Z Dev Center, Real People Real SEO. None of them are dominant nationally; several are generalist SEO shops with AEO landing pages. This is the most winnable category in the entire audit.
4. **Founder-name search returns confusion, not authority.** "Jake Beach AEO" returns disambiguation with unrelated Jake Beaches (insurance, law, CNA). No entity graph resolves Jake Beach → AEO Listings founder. This is a knowledge-graph gap that Wikipedia-style anchoring (LinkedIn Company page + Person schema + a few outbound corroborating mentions) will fix within 60–90 days.
5. **The site does not appear in a direct-branded search.** WebSearch for "aeolistings.ai AEO Listings" returned zero result mentioning the domain. This isn't a citation problem — this is an indexing/discoverability problem. Fix priority #1.

**Do not sugarcoat interpretation:** this is the expected shape of a Month 0 audit for an agency with a young domain, 9 blog posts, and no external corroboration surface. The blog posts on aeolistings.ai are strong content but they are pointed inward at the site. AI answer engines don't cite content that lives only on your own domain for competitive category prompts — they cite content about you that lives on other domains. That's the roadmap.

## F. Gap analysis by pattern (5-pattern frame from aeolistings.ai blog corpus)

**1. Entity signals — SEVERE gap.** #1 root cause. Direct-brand query "aeolistings.ai AEO Listings" returns zero brand mentions. Founder query "Jake Beach AEO" returns four unrelated Jake Beaches. Schema.org root graph works internally, but no external corroboration to trigger AI answer-engine confidence. **Fix priority: highest.**

**2. Corroboration — SEVERE gap.** #2 root cause and the unlock for Category 1 prompts. Every generic AEO agency prompt is answered from listicle content on third-party domains. aeolistings.ai does not appear on any of them. First Page Sage, OGTool, NoGood are on 10+ lists each. aeolistings.ai is on zero. **Fix: get on those lists.**

**3. Category specificity — MODERATE gap, but MOST winnable.** The "AEO for home service contractors" vertical has a shallow competitive field and no dominant national brand. Phoenix/Mesa geo has an even shallower field. Category-specific service pages + regional corroboration can win these categories in 60–90 days.

**4. Passage retrievability — LIKELY OK, unverifiable at this scale.** Blog posts appear structured for extractability, but without paid seats to test whether specific passages are getting pulled, this is untestable in baseline.

**5. Recency — NOT the bottleneck yet.** Content published ~2x/week. Recency is fine. Only becomes the bottleneck after entity and corroboration are solved.
