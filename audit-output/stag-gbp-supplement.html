90-day rollout

A steady, weekly cadence —
not a redesign.

Sequenced so the highest-leverage moves happen in week one; cadence-building fills weeks 2–4; velocity compounds 30–90.

Week 1 · Quick wins
Foundation
  • Confirm primary category = Electrician (most specific)
  • Add 3–4 secondaries (EV Charging Station Contractor, Electrical Installation, Generator Installation)
  • Reply to all existing reviews across Google, Yelp, BBB, HomeAdvisor
  • Upload 30+ existing job-site photos (panel work, EV installs, troubleshooting)
  • Add identity + service attributes (family-owned, free estimates, language)
  • Update business description with Mesa + East Valley + EV / panel wedge
  • Seed 5–7 owner-posted Q&A entries (EV cost, panel upgrade, breaker tripping)
Weeks 2–4 · Activate
Build cadence
  • Wire review-request workflow to Google — move HomeAdvisor reviewers + new customers
  • Begin 1–2 Google Posts / week (panel jobs, EV installs, monsoon advisories)
  • Add 5–8 Products entries (EV install, panel upgrade, troubleshoot visit) with starting-at pricing
  • Photo cadence: 2–3 new per week, geotagged
  • Confirm service-area: Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler, Tempe, Scottsdale, Apache Junction, Queen Creek, Phoenix
Days 30–90 · Compound
Ramp velocity

  • Sustained photo cadence — target 50+ total in 90 days
  • Q&A monitoring <48 hours
  • Track Local Pack for "electrician Mesa AZ", "EV charger installer Phoenix", "panel upgrade Mesa", "Tesla Wall Connector installer near me"
