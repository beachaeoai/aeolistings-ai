## E. Where competitors are winning

**Mariano & Co. (6 citations on non-branded queries)** — They win Chandler-area custom + remodel + cost intent. The specific content assets being cited:
- `marianoco.com/custom-home-building/` — generic custom-home-building pillar
- `marianoco.com/custom-home-building/cost-of-luxury-home-in-mesa-arizona/` — Mesa-specific cost page
- `marianoco.com/custom-home-building/mesa-az-tips/` — Mesa-specific timeline + tips
- `marianoco.com/chandler-home-remodeling-services/` — Chandler remodel pillar
- `marianoco.com/` — homepage with "We Build Custom Homes Too" framing

The pattern: **city-specific landing pages with cost or timeline data in the page itself**. That's what LLMs cite. Cruz currently has service-line pages (custom home, spec home) but not city × service-line × intent pages.

**Rodeo Construction (4 citations)** — Wins Glendale + West Valley + how-to-choose. Their Glendale-specific page (`arizona.rodeoconstruction.com/custom-home-builder/glendale/`) is purpose-built and dominates a city Cruz also serves. Their geographic-coverage statement is being repeated verbatim by Google's AI.

**Forte Homes (2 citations)** — Wins on Chandler "best of" lists and the 2026 highest-rated query. Forte's lead is brand age + decades-of-Chandler-history — a hard moat to attack head-on, but not insurmountable on cost / process / specialty queries where Forte doesn't appear.

**Aggregators / third parties also winning citation share** — Hughes Development, Trinity Homes AZ, AFT Construction, Boxwell Homes, Cullum Homes, Starwood Custom Homes, and Phoenix Home Remodeling all earn multiple citations across the prompt set without being on the named-competitor list. Trend tracking should expand to include these as informal competitors.

## F. Recommended content moves (next 30 days)

Tied to the gaps surfaced above, in priority order:

1. **Fix the Matthew Gallego mis-attribution (Week 1).** Build a `/about/matthew-gallego/` page with `Person` schema, employment history, founding-of-Cruz date, photo, and LinkedIn link. Push fresh LinkedIn content tagging Cruz Development. Goal: out-rank the stale ZoomInfo entry that's currently telling LLMs Matthew works at Starwood. **This is the highest-ROI single action in the report — it stops bleeding before it starts.**

2. **Build a Chandler-luxury-custom-home pillar (Week 1–2).** Target prompt #1 directly. Page must include: cost data ($/sq ft ranges from the Cruz cost ladder), timeline data (X–Y months specific to Chandler permits), HOA approval workflow, lot recommendations in Chandler, and a "why choose vertically integrated over a production builder" section. Mirror the structure of `marianoco.com/custom-home-building/` but with Cruz's in-house differentiator pushed harder. Add `LocalBusiness` + `FAQ` + `HowTo` schema.

3. **Build a "vertically integrated custom home builder" content asset (Week 2).** Cruz already says "no subcontractors, all in-house" — but it's a positioning claim, not yet a citation-earning piece of content. Write a `/why-cruz/in-house-team/` page that defines vertical integration in custom home building, names each in-house discipline (architect, designer, project manager, finish trades), shows the org chart, explains the time + quality + transparency benefit with specific examples. This is the angle competitors *can't* easily copy. Targets prompts #35, #36.

4. **Build city-specific cost pages for Chandler, Mesa, Glendale (Week 2–3).** Three landing pages, each ~1,500 words, each containing the local $/sq ft band, local permit fees, local lot cost benchmarks, and Cruz's cost ladder. These are the queries Mariano (Mesa) and Rodeo (Glendale) currently win — the structure to copy is already visible in the citation evidence. Targets #9, #11, #12, #13, #15.

5. **Strengthen the existing "How to Build a House in Phoenix" guide (Week 3).** This page is *already* in the link list for prompt #28 — it's the closest to a citation in the entire non-branded set. Add: more first-person founder voice, named local sub-stages (Maricopa County permit timing, ROC-licensed inspections), a downloadable checklist (citation magnet for LLMs that prefer linkable artifacts), and `HowTo` schema. Goal: convert link-list-only to a named citation by Month 3.

6. **Publish 3 third-party review-collection assets (Week 3–4).** The branded query #33 explicitly flagged that *"the search results don't contain specific customer reviews"* — this is the gating factor for trust-vetting. Push existing happy clients to leave reviews on Google Business Profile, Houzz, and Better Business Bureau (the BBB profile is currently for the Ohio Cruz Development entity, which compounds the disambiguation problem). Goal by Month 3: at least 15 reviews on GBP and a clean AZ-specific BBB profile.

**Deferred for Month 2+:** Spec home pillar refresh, Santa Fe / Pueblo specialty pages (low-volume but uncontested), HOA-approval guide for Mesa.
