## A. Methodology and surfaces tested

This is the **baseline** citation report. No prior month exists — every line below is a fresh data point that future months will be compared against.

**Prompt set:** 40 buyer-intent prompts spanning seven intent buckets:
- Direct ranking / list intent (8)
- Cost / specification intent (8)
- Comparison intent (5)
- Specialty / EV intent (5)
- Process intent (5)
- Troubleshooting intent (5)
- Trust / vetting intent (4)

**Surfaces tested:**
- **Google AI Overview / Web (directly tested)** — Each prompt run as a Google search via WebSearch. The summary blocks returned reflect the named-business citations Google's AI surface produces for these queries. This is the highest-trafficked AI search surface and the one most relevant to a Mesa homeowner.
- **Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini (manual capture required)** — Direct programmatic access to these surfaces is not available in this environment. They are flagged "manual capture required" in the citation table. We recommend the client (or Aeolistings ops) capture these monthly using a private window login on each surface so the dataset is consistent month over month.

**Competitor watch list** (from the original Stag Electric audit):
- **Castle Electrical & Lighting** (Scottsdale) — Tesla Certified + Qmerit credentials, the closest credential-equivalent rival
- **Turn It On Electric** — volume leader in panel/EV work
- **Just Right Electric** — similar size + service mix to Stag
Each of these is flagged below when surfaced.

**What we are NOT measuring:** Google Local Pack (map results), paid ads, organic blue-link rankings. Those are tracked separately. This report is *citation behavior in AI-generated answers* only.

## C. Per-prompt findings (with verbatim citation evidence)

Stag is not cited on prompts #1–36 or #39–40. Detail below is grouped by intent bucket; the trust prompts (#37–38) get full verbatim treatment because those are the two wins.

**Ranking / list (#1–8).** Stag absent across all eight. The Wire Guy Electric, Dolce Electric, Meade Electrical, ACT Electric, and Scheid Electric trade the top-of-list slots across Mesa-specific queries; T&K, Watt Masters, Apollo, Mr. Electric, and State Electrical lead Phoenix-metro queries. Notably Scheid Electric is credentialed as "Tesla-certified" inline (#3) — a direct credential signal Stag does not have. EV (#7) and panel-upgrade (#8) ranking lists — Stag's two stated specialties — both omit Stag entirely. Sources powering these answers: Yelp, Angi, Expertise.com, HomeAdvisor, ThreeBestRated, ContractorListsHQ.

**Cost / specification (#9–16).** Stag absent across all eight. AI surfaces these pricing bands: panel upgrade Phoenix $1,240–$2,066 (or $1,500–$3,000 broad); panel upgrade Mesa $316–$457 low end / $850–$4,000 comprehensive; 200A upgrade AZ $1,500–$4,000; Level 2 EV charger AZ $1,100–$1,200 (or $300–$3,500 broad); Tesla Wall Connector Phoenix $1,000–$4,000 ballpark; whole-house rewire AZ $377–$1,869 average / $2,000–$9,000+ for larger jobs; troubleshooting $50–$100/hr standard, $150–$300/hr emergency. Citations: Homeyou, Angi, Manta, RES Arizona, Promotcher, Livewire, HighDesertEnergy. **No Mesa contractor is anchoring the Mesa-specific pricing answer with verifiable local data — that's a clean opportunity.**

**Comparison (#17–21).** Stag absent across all five. George Brazil owns "100 amp vs 200 amp panel Arizona" (#17) with their AZ-specific panel-size guide. Dolce Electric owns "panel upgrade vs sub-panel Mesa" (#20) with two separate Mesa landing pages. The Wire Guy Electric owns "DIY vs licensed electrician Arizona" (#21) with a dedicated blog post quoting Phoenix code enforcement verbatim. The product/spec question on Tesla vs ChargePoint (#18) and the hardwired vs plug-in question (#19) surface only national reviewers and brand sites — limited contractor opportunity there.

**Specialty / EV (#22–26).** Stag absent across all five. **Castle Electric (watch list) IS cited on #22** — verbatim: "Castle Electric — Specializes in installing top-of-the-line Tesla Gen 3 Wall Connectors that turn your garage into a high-powered charging station." Other competitors winning EV citations: Black Platinum Solar & Electric, Watt Masters, Bleuwave, Livewire, Mason Pro Services, Charge Home Solutions, Rizzo Electric USA, Tesla Electric (Goodyear). The Tesla certification skepticism note in #23 ("'Tesla Certified' is often considered irrelevant marketing") slightly dampens the credential moat for everyone — but doesn't help Stag who has no credential to dampen.

**Process (#27–31).** Stag absent across all five. The Wire Guy Electric again wins #27 with a "2025 permit guide." The permit-cost-Mesa question (#30) surfaces an explicit AI-side data gap — "the specific permit costs for Mesa are not detailed in the available search results" — meaning whoever publishes the answer first earns the citation. **Troubleshooting (#32–36).** Stag absent across all five.
