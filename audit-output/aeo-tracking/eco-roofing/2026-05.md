## F. Recommended content moves for Month 1 (June 2026)

Six prioritized moves. Each is a publishable content asset with a target prompt cluster.

### 1. Publish a year-stamped 2026 Phoenix Valley Roofing Cost Guide
**Target prompts:** 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16 (cost cluster — currently 0/8 cited).
**Format:** pillar page at `/cost-guide-2026/` (or similar) + 8 supporting blog posts, one per prompt. Include: tile replacement Mesa, tile repair Phoenix, foam $/sq ft (this is Eco Roof's specialty — own it), reroof Mesa, AZ 2026 averages by material, cool vs traditional, East Valley labor rates, tile vs shingle.
**Why:** the cost cluster is the largest content gap (8 of 8 prompts uncited), the SERP is currently won by Beach Roofing's 2026 cost guide (a single page) and a fractured collection of generic cost-calculator pages. A well-structured Eco Roof guide can claim the 2026-stamped position.
**Citation hook to include:** specific dollar figures with effective dates ("As of April 2026, tile reroof in Mesa..."), a stated methodology (data source, sample size of recent jobs), and Eric Perry quoted as the named expert.

### 2. Build a "Tile vs Foam vs Shingle vs Flat for AZ" comparison cluster
**Target prompts:** 17, 18, 19 (comparison cluster — currently 0/3 cited; 4 and 5 if specialty prompts are included = 0/5).
**Format:** one pillar page comparing all four roof types for Phoenix climate, plus three child posts on the specific pairings.
**Why:** Eco Roof is one of the few AZ contractors that installs all four (tile/foam/flat/shingle/rolled) — that breadth is a unique authority signal. Currently no AZ contractor is consolidating the comparison; Roofing All Stars, Lyons, JBS each cover fragments.
**Citation hook:** include an objective decision matrix (climate zone, slope, budget tier, lifespan goal) — AI Overview loves matrices and tends to cite them verbatim.

### 3. Publish a "Phoenix monsoon tile-leak diagnostic guide"
**Target prompts:** 37 ("Why is my tile roof leaking in monsoon season Phoenix"), supporting 21, 38, 40.
