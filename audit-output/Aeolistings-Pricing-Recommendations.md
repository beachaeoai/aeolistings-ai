# Aeolistings Pricing — Competitive Audit & Recommendations

*April 2026 · v1.0 · Internal*

A line-by-line validation of Aeolistings' published pricing against publicly available competitor pricing in the local SEO, AEO/GEO, contractor-marketing, and home-builder-marketing categories. Research basis: 35 cited sources, 2026-current.

---

## Executive summary

Aeolistings sits in the **"premium-craft solo / boutique"** tier — well above the cheap-template / Townsquare / Fiverr layer, comparable to specialist boutiques (Hook Agency, Builder Funnel), and below the enterprise contractor shops (Blue Corona, Sterling Sky, BlackStorm enterprise tier).

Most line items are fairly priced or slightly underpriced. Two clear outliers:

1. **AEO retainer is significantly underpriced** vs. the emerging AEO/GEO market. The category is producing *upward* pricing pressure, not commoditization, and Aeolistings' $1,500/mo standard rate is below the published floor of credible AEO/GEO retainers ($2,500–$3,000/mo).
2. **Social media management at $1,500/mo for ~65 posts/month** is below market for any package that includes original video. Either cap to static-only or raise to $2,500/mo.

The pricing changes recommended below would lift recurring revenue by ~$1,000/mo per client without losing competitiveness — Aeolistings would still be the cheapest credible AEO specialist in the contractor vertical.

## Recommended changes

### Raise (4 items)

| Item | Current | Recommended | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AEO Retainer (founding)** | $1,000/mo | **$1,750/mo** | Below market floor at $1,000. $1,750 still cheapest credible AEO specialist. |
| **AEO Retainer (standard)** | $1,500/mo | **$2,500/mo** | Sterling Sky floor is $3,000/mo for home services. WebFX, Digital Elevator, RevvGrowth all peg comparable scope at $3,000–$5,000/mo. Even at $2,500 you're cheapest-credible. |
| **Authority Content Pack** | $2,750 | **$3,750** | At $0.40–$1.00/word agency rates, 5 × 1,500-word pillars + FAQ schema is $3,000–$7,500 floor. Cluster pricing in market is $3,000–$10,000. Current price reads as freelancer, not specialist. |
| **AEO Foundation** | $1,500 | **$1,750** | Modest bump; mid-market is $2,000–$3,000 for a real schema rollout. |

**Combined revenue impact per founding client:** +$750/mo recurring + $1,000 one-time = **~$10,000/yr increase per retainer client.**

### Hold

Website ($7,500), GBP ($1,250), Rapid Rescue ($2,500), Hub Page ($1,250), City Pages ($3,000), Social Foundation ($750), Service Page Bundle ($1,750), Trust Signal Buildout ($1,750).

### Decision needed: Social Media Management

Current $1,500/mo for ~65 posts/month across FB/IG/TikTok is structurally unprofitable per Sprout Social, WebFX, and 12AM Agency 2026 pricing guides if any short-form video is included. Two paths:

- **Path A:** Cap explicitly at static-content-only (no original video) — keep at $1,500/mo. Add a tier upgrade for video at $2,500/mo.
- **Path B:** Restructure to $2,500/mo flat, with video included. Aligns with market.

Recommendation: **Path A** — preserve a low-friction entry tier for cost-sensitive prospects, with a clearly named upgrade.

### Possibly missing line items

Three line items competitors sell separately that Aeolistings could add:

1. **Citation buildout / NAP cleanup** — BrightLocal at $3.20/citation, Whitespark Listing Cleanup $20–$999. Most agencies charge $300–$750 for one-time NAP audit + 25–50 citation submission. Suggested price: **$500–$750 one-time** (or rolled into Rapid Rescue).
2. **Standalone AI Visibility Audit** — GenOptima, Stackmatix, SEM Nexus all sell this as a foot-in-the-door at $1,500–$5,000. You're already producing these (Cruz, Eco Roofing, Stag); productize at **$1,500–$2,500**.
3. **Review-acquisition system standalone** — currently bundled into the AEO retainer. Comp shops sell this at $250–$500/mo or $500–$1,000 one-time. Worth surfacing as a line item for clients who don't want the full retainer. Suggested: **$500 one-time setup + $250/mo recurring**.

## Packaging strategy notes

**Comp pattern:** Most contractor-marketing agencies sell 3-tier packages (starter / growth / enterprise). Aeolistings' à-la-carte approach reads as boutique-consultant — that's not bad (telegraphs senior work), but be aware contractor prospects often shop in package format. Consider adding 3 named bundles for clearer comparison shopping:

- **Foundation Bundle** (~$8,750 one-time + $1,750/mo) — Website + GBP + AEO Retainer
- **Growth Bundle** (~$12,500 one-time + $2,000/mo) — Foundation + Authority Content Pack + Review System
- **Authority Bundle** (~$18,000 one-time + $3,000/mo) — Growth + Trust Signal Buildout + Social Foundation + Social Mgmt

These layer on top of the à-la-carte option, not replace it. Founding-client discount stacks.

**Founding-client refinement:** Current 25% off one-time work is generous. Add a parallel hook for retainer hesitancy: **25% off the first 3 months of retainer for founding clients.** Common SaaS-style risk-reduction signal.

## Market context (one paragraph)

The AEO/GEO category in 2026 is producing **upward** pricing pressure, not commoditization. GenOptima, RevvGrowth, Stackmatix, and Digital Elevator all describe a "black box" market with rising rates as demand outstrips capacity. Generic GEO agencies are pricing for B2B SaaS at $3,000+/mo. Aeolistings' opportunity is to be **the credible AEO specialist in the contractor vertical** at a price point below the generalist GEO shops. $2,000–$2,500/mo is the right strategic price — cheap enough vs. a generic GEO shop, but priced like an AEO specialist not a local-SEO commodity provider.
