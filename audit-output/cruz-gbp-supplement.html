Critical gaps — ranked by impact

Ten fixes that change Local Pack visibility.

In priority order. The first three are recoverable inside week one and account for the largest share of the visibility lift.

  • NAP inconsistency across surfacesHouzz says San Tan Valley; the website displays no address; the GBP setup is unverified. Inconsistent NAP actively suppresses local-search ranking. Reconciling it across all surfaces is the single highest-impact GBP fix. Critical
  • 619 (San Diego) phone on Arizona-targeted businessOut-of-state area codes are a quiet trust deduction for in-state buyers and a soft signal Google's local algorithm reads. An AZ tracking number alongside the existing owner cell solves it without changing operations. Critical
  • Wrong primary category likely set"Custom Home Builder" is the most specific match and the strongest Local Pack signal. If Cruz is set to a broader category ("General Contractor"), the entire profile underperforms. Verify and reset on day one. Critical
  • No Service-Area Business configuration verifiedFor a builder serving Chandler / Mesa / Glendale from a San Tan Valley address, the GBP must be set as SAB with all three cities explicitly inside the radius. A storefront pin in San Tan Valley alone won't rank for Chandler queries. High
  • No Houzz "Best Of" badge pursuitFor luxury custom home builders, Houzz is a second search engine. Mariano has 15 Houzz Awards. A reviews + projects push positions Cruz for Best of Houzz 2027. High
  • Name-collision risk with cruzcruzdevelopment.comTwo distinct "Cruz Development" entities exist. Search engines occasionally conflate them. A schema sameAs array linking the canonical website, GBP, Houzz, and IG cleans up disambiguation. High
  • Brad Leavitt podcast appearance not surfaced on GBPThe strongest existing third-party authority signal Cruz has — Matthew Gallego on a respected industry podcast — should appear in posts, attributes, and the business description. Currently invisible. High
  • No NAHB / HBACA / NARI / BBB accreditationForte is HBACA + NAHB + BBB. Each is a directory citation, a trust badge, and an AEO factual signal. Entry-level reputation play Cruz isn't running. Medium
  • Posts cadence + photo cadence unknownBuilder GBPs that post 4+ project photos per month dramatically outperform static profiles. Cadence needs to be verified manually and ramped to weekly. Medium
  • Reviews program — quantity, recency, response rateAll three are independent ranking factors and all three need to be measured. Target >25 Google reviews, 100% owner response, and a steady velocity of new reviews. Medium

Sequenced so the highest-leverage changes happen in week one, the cadence-building work fills weeks 2–4, and the velocity ramp compounds across days 30–90.

Foundation

Bottom line

The GBP is the surface where buyers verify what the website claims. Right now they can't.

Cruz's website says one thing about who they are. The GBP says something different. Houzz says a third. The phone says San Diego. Once those four surfaces tell the same coherent story — credentialed AZ luxury builder, named founder, real address, podcast-cited industry voice — Cruz becomes legible to both AI assistants and buyers. That's the whole 90-day arc.
