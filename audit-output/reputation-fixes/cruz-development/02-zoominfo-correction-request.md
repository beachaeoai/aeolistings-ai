## Why this matters more than just publishing the new bio page

Publishing `/about/matthew-gallego/` is the **defensive** move — it gives Google AI Overview a better source to quote. Submitting the ZoomInfo correction is the **offensive** move — it removes the bad source from the underlying citation corpus entirely.

Together, they're meaningfully more durable than either alone. The bio page might take 30–60 days to outrank ZoomInfo on its own. With ZoomInfo updated, the timeline compresses substantially because there's no longer a competing wrong answer in the data layer AI assistants are pulling from.
