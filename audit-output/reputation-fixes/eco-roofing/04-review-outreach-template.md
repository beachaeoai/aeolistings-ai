# Review Outreach — HomeAdvisor → Google

**Goal:** Move existing satisfied HomeAdvisor reviewers (and any other already-positive customer relationships) to also leave Google reviews. The complaint quote currently dominating branded queries on AI Overview gets less weight as the volume of recent positive Google reviews grows. Target: **15+ new Google reviews in 60 days.**

**Why this matters more than just publishing the new commitment page:** Publishing the commitment page is the **defensive** move — better source for AI Overview to quote. Generating new Google reviews is the **offensive** move — more positive signals in the citation corpus directly competing with the complaint. The complaint quote currently weights ~1/3 of the answer because it's one of the most-recent specific signals about Eco Roofing. Adding 15+ recent positive Google reviews materially shifts that ratio.

**Effort:** ~3 hours of Eric's time to identify the recipient list and personalize the first batch. Subsequent batches can be sent by anyone on the team.

---

## How this works mechanically

Google AI Overview's "Is Eco Roofing Solutions reputable" answer pulls from:

1. **The complaint text** (currently ~1/3 of the answer's weight) — single source, recent, specific, named
2. **Generic positive descriptors** (BBB accreditation, HomeAdvisor rating) — multiple sources, but generic
3. **Real recent customer-voice content** — currently underweighted because Eco Roofing has only ~5 Google reviews despite hundreds-implied across other platforms

The fix isn't *more* reviews on more platforms. It's specifically more reviews on **Google** — because that's the source AI Overview weights heaviest for branded reputation queries.

## Step 3 — Get the Google review short link right

**This step is critical.** The link must take the reviewer directly to the review form, not to the general business profile.

To generate Eco Roofing's Google review short link:

1. Open the Eco Roofing GBP in Google Maps
2. Click "Get more reviews" (button on profile management view at https://business.google.com/reviews/)
3. Copy the short link Google generates — format: `https://g.page/r/<placeId>/review`
4. Test the link in incognito — confirm it goes straight to the 5-star rating + comment form

Alternative: use Google's place-finder tool at https://developers.google.com/places/place-id to find Eco Roofing's place ID, then construct: `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=<PLACE_ID>`

**Don't use a link to the GBP itself** — review-completion drops 50%+ when reviewers have to find the review button.

## Step 4 — Sequence the sends

Don't blast all 30 at once. Sequence in three waves over 21 days:

- **Wave 1 (Day 1):** Version A from Eric to the 5 strongest relationships
- **Wave 2 (Day 7):** Version C from team member to the 10 HomeAdvisor reviewers
- **Wave 3 (Day 14):** Version B from team member to the remaining 15

Why staggered:
- Avoids the "everyone reviewed in 24 hours" pattern that Google flags as suspicious (and may strip)
- Gives Eric personal-touch credit on the closest relationships
- Spreads team workload

## Step 5 — Follow-up note when a review lands

Send a simple thank-you within 48 hours of each new review:

```
[First name] — saw the Google review come through. Means a lot.
Thank you for taking the time.

Eric  (or [sender name])
Eco Roofing Solutions
```

Don't ask for anything else. Don't try to nudge a 4-star review up to 5. The thank-you is just for the relationship — and it's the move that produces the second review six months from now.

## Step 6 — Owner-respond to every review (positive AND negative)

This is part of GBP best practice (see `gbp-optimize` skill, Step 12) but it matters extra during a reputation-recovery push:

- **5-star:** Specific thanks naming the work + a forward-looking line. 30–60 words.
- **4-star:** Specific thanks + acknowledgment of what could have been better + improvement note.
- **1–3 star:** Specific empathy + the fact pattern from Eco Roofing's perspective + Eric's direct contact + offer to make it right. 80–150 words.

Response rate and response speed are both Google ranking signals. Aim for 100% response within 48 hours.

See `review-response` skill for templates.

## What NOT to do

- **Don't offer anything for reviews** — discounts, gift cards, future-project credit. Google prohibits this and will strip the reviews + can suspend the GBP.
- **Don't write the review for them.** Templated reviews are flagged.
- **Don't ask for "5 stars"** — ask for "an honest review." The 5 stars come if the experience earned them.
- **Don't send to customers with unresolved issues** — that produces a 1-star review you didn't have before.
- **Don't re-ask** if someone doesn't respond. One ask is the right ask.
- **Don't outsource to a review-aggregation service.** They batch-blast and get accounts suspended. Slow personal outreach is what works.
