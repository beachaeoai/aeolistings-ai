## Also in the market (evaluated, not recommended for most buyers)

Several Phoenix-area shops advertise AEO services but the offering appears to be a landing page bolted onto existing SEO/dev work with no distinct methodology, dedicated team, or citation-tracking capability. We're not going to name names — the audit tools do that in the sales conversation. If you're evaluating a Phoenix AEO agency not on this list, three sanity checks:

1. **Do they track citation share, or do they track keyword rankings?** These are different things and the difference is the whole discipline.
2. **Do they publish a methodology or framework?** Not required, but a shop that can't articulate their process usually doesn't have one.
3. **Do they show you a citation audit before they ask for a signed agreement?** We do this on every sales call — it should be table stakes.
