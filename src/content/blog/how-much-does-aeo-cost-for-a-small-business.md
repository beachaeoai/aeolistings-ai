**For a local service business in 2026, expect AEO engagements to run between $1,000 and $5,000 per month on a recurring basis, with a separate one-time foundation cost of $3,000 to $15,000 depending on whether a new website is part of the scope. The under-$1,000 tier is almost always a citation-tracking dashboard plus light content — useful as a measurement layer but not enough work to move citation share on its own. Anything over $5,000 per month for a single-location local business is generally either including paid media, multiple geographies, or markup on outsourced production. The honest pricing range for the actual work is narrower than the agency landscape suggests.**

The price ambiguity is part of the problem. Most agencies in this category don't publish numbers, which makes comparison shopping hard and gives space for a small number of operators to charge enterprise rates for solo-practitioner work. Here's what's actually inside each tier.

## What you're paying for, by tier

The line items for any honest AEO engagement break into three categories:

1. **One-time foundation work** — a new or rebuilt website, schema markup, Google Business Profile claim and rebuild, initial content production, citation cleanup. Lasts the engagement; not repeated.
2. **Recurring authority and operations work** — monthly prompt-set tracking, blog content production, GBP post cadence, review velocity, monthly reporting, quarterly strategy review.
3. **Optional specialty work** — social media management, paid media, video production. Bundled or à-la-carte depending on the shop.

The recurring tier is where the monthly number lives. The foundation tier is what creates the asymmetry between agencies — same monthly retainer, very different first-month deliverable.

## Recurring monthly retainer — what each price point actually buys

### Under $1,000/month

What you usually get: a citation-tracking tool subscription (often resold from BrightLocal or similar), one or two short blog posts per month, light schema or GBP touch-ups. What you don't get: enough content production to move citation share, dedicated strategic attention, or active prompt-set monitoring across all five major answer engines.

This tier works as a *measurement* layer if you're producing content elsewhere. As a standalone AEO engagement, it doesn't usually pencil — the volume isn't there to compete in any meaningful local market.

### $1,000–$2,500/month

What you usually get: 40 to 60 buyer-intent prompts tracked monthly across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overview; 8 to 13 blog posts per month written for direct AI retrieval; review-velocity system; weekly performance updates; quarterly strategy reviews. This is the floor for credible AEO work on a single local business.

Most engagements that produce visible citation lift inside 90 days sit in this band. Variance within the range comes from blog volume, depth of GBP work, and how aggressive the off-site corroboration push is.

### $2,500–$5,000/month

What you usually get: everything in the lower tier plus content-cluster production (cost pillars, comparison pages, city-by-city sub-pages), more aggressive review acquisition campaigns, social media management on one to three platforms, and proactive crisis monitoring (catching mis-attributions, surfaced negative reviews, brand-confusion incidents in AI answers).

This is the right band for businesses in competitive metros (Phoenix, Dallas, Atlanta) or in saturated service categories (HVAC, roofing in any major metro). It's also the right band when the business has a meaningful brand-positioning component the AEO work has to express, not just describe.

### Above $5,000/month

You're almost always paying for one of: paid media, multi-location coverage, enterprise compliance overhead, or markup on outsourced production. None of those is wrong — but make sure you know which one before you sign.

## One-time foundation cost — narrower than you'd think

The foundation cost is where you'll see the widest agency variance because the work is genuinely different per client:

- **No new website needed** — usually $2,500 to $6,000 total for GBP optimization, schema rollout, FAQ rewrite, trust-signal buildout, and an authority content pack. About a month of work.
- **New website required** — add $5,000 to $10,000 for a custom 5- to 9-page build with AEO-optimized copy, schema on every page, and integrated content infrastructure. About six to eight weeks of work end-to-end including the recurring work that overlaps.
- **Multi-location or franchise** — meaningfully higher; typically scoped per location plus a brand-level layer.

If an agency proposal has the foundation work blended into the recurring retainer without a clear one-time line item, that's worth questioning. Usually it means either the foundation is being amortized (you pay forever for work that should have been done once) or the foundation isn't actually being done.

## What to be skeptical of in proposals

Three patterns to push back on:

- **"AI ranking" as a measured outcome.** AI doesn't rank — it cites. Anyone offering to improve your "AI ranking" is either using sloppy language or measuring something cheaper than citation share.
- **No discount-by-tenure structure.** Most credible operators offer founding-client or early-engagement discounts because they're explicitly trying to build category authority. A flat enterprise price for a small local business is usually agency markup on cheaper underlying production.
- **Bundled pricing with no line items.** A single monthly number with no breakdown of what's included makes scope creep invisible and renegotiation impossible.

## The pricing question you should actually be asking

Not "how much does AEO cost," but "how much does AEO cost *for my specific business*." A roofer in a 5-million-person metro with three direct competitors all running active AEO is a very different engagement from a custom home builder in a 200,000-person metro with no AEO-active competitors at all. The first needs aggressive content production and competitive monitoring; the second can win on foundation work alone for the first six to nine months.

Honest agencies will quote a different number for each. Be wary of the agency that quotes the same monthly number regardless of what they hear on the discovery call.
