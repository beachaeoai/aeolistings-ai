**In July 2026 we ran our standard Month 0 citation audit on our own agency — the same 29-prompt baseline we run for every client. The result: zero citations. Not one tested buyer-intent prompt, from "best AEO agency" to "AEO agency in Phoenix" to our own founder's name, returned aeolistings.ai in the answer. We're publishing the result because we tell clients a zero baseline is normal, diagnosable, and fixable — and the only honest way to say that is to show our own zero and what we're doing about it.**

One of our four operating principles is that we have to pass our own test: if this site doesn't eventually get recommended by the AI chatbots when someone asks for an AEO agency, we have no business selling that outcome. This post is the "before" picture, on the record, so the after — whatever it turns out to be — is verifiable against something.

## What we ran

The same protocol we use for clients: a fixed prompt set across six categories — generic agency prompts ("best AEO agency 2026"), geographic prompts ("AEO agency in Mesa Arizona"), vertical prompts ("AEO for HVAC contractors"), problem prompts ("how do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT"), definitional prompts ("what is llms.txt"), and founder-name prompts. Sixteen of the 29 prompts were directly testable on Google AI Overview at baseline; the remaining engines get manual capture to complete the Month 0 record.

Sixteen tested. Zero citations. And two findings that stung more than the count:

**Our own brand search didn't surface us.** A search for "aeolistings.ai AEO Listings" produced an AI answer that said, in effect, *this website doesn't appear in the results* — and then listed other marketing companies. The domain wasn't in the AI retrieval pool for its own name.

**Our founder's name resolved to strangers.** "Jake Beach AEO" returned a disambiguation across several unrelated people named Jake Beach — an insurance-company employee, an attorney — with no connection drawn to this agency at all.

## Why a zero baseline is the expected shape

We say this to clients constantly, and it applies identically to us: a young domain with good content and no external corroboration scores zero on competitive prompts, because **AI answer engines don't cite what you say about yourself — they cite what others say about you.**

At audit time we had a technically clean site: full Schema.org entity graph, retrievable answer-first content, eleven blog posts, consistent NAP, verified everything. None of it was the bottleneck. The audit showed every generic "best AEO agency" prompt being answered from third-party listicles — First Page Sage, agency roundups on Respona and Minuttia, a LinkedIn Pulse post — and we appeared on none of them. The agencies being cited weren't running better websites. They were on more lists.

That's the whole diagnosis, and it maps to two of the [five patterns](/blog/why-chatgpt-recommends-your-competitor-instead-of-you) we use for every client: an entity gap (the brand-search and founder-name failures) and a corroboration gap (the listicle absence). Retrievability and recency — the on-site patterns — were fine. Which is itself a useful data point: **you can do everything right on your own domain and still score zero.** On-site work is necessary. It is nowhere near sufficient.

## The response plan we're running on ourselves

The same sequence we'd prescribe a client with this exact audit, in priority order:

**1. List placement outreach.** Pitching the specific listicles the audit showed feeding AI answers in our category — the publications actually cited by Google AI Overview on "best AEO agency" prompts. Target: placement on two of the five pitched within the first cycle. This is the highest-leverage move because it attacks the exact sources the answers are built from. (The method became its own post: [how to get on the lists AI actually reads](/blog/how-to-get-on-the-best-of-lists-ai-actually-reads).)

**2. A syndicated press release.** One wire release, entity-exact copy, with the month-to-month retainer as the announceable hook. We watched a competitor earn national vertical citations from essentially one well-aimed release; the mechanics are in [the press release piece](/blog/the-press-release-is-back-as-an-ai-corroboration-play).

**3. Founder entity repair.** A dedicated profile page with `ProfilePage` schema pointed at the Person entity, expanded `sameAs` links, and byline-building so "Jake Beach" plus anything AEO-adjacent resolves here instead of to an insurance adjuster in another state. Knowledge-graph repair is slow — we expect this one in months, not weeks.

**4. Category-specific pages for the winnable prompts.** The audit showed the geographic prompts ("AEO agency in Phoenix") had the shallowest competition — mostly generalist SEO shops with AEO landing pages bolted on. We shipped a dedicated Phoenix/Mesa page and a home-services vertical page targeting the prompt language buyers actually use.

**5. Monthly re-measurement, published.** Same prompt set, every month, deltas against this zero. That's the part most agencies skip — and the part that makes the rest falsifiable.

## What we expect, on the record

Our own timeline guidance says foundation signals register in four to eight weeks, visibility compounds over two to three months, and durable citation share takes six to twelve months. We're holding ourselves to that curve publicly: corroboration placements should start appearing in AI answers within a couple of months of landing; the founder-name fix will lag; the competitive "best agency" prompts — where we're up against entrenched national players on dozens of lists — are the twelve-month fight, and honestly may be longer.

We'll publish the follow-up when there's real delta data — including if the news is bad. A transparency post that only ships when the numbers are good is marketing with extra steps.

## What this means if you're a local business

Three things transfer directly:

1. **Get the baseline before judging anything.** Zero is a starting measurement, not a verdict. What matters is knowing it, in prompt-level detail, so progress is attributable. If you want to run your own, [the DIY method is here](/blog/how-to-check-if-chatgpt-recommends-your-business).
2. **A good website is the floor, not the strategy.** Our site was clean and it bought us nothing on competitive prompts. The citations live in the corroboration layer — reviews, lists, mentions, news — and that's where the work is.
3. **Distrust anyone who won't show you their own numbers.** We're an AEO agency that just published a zero. Ask any agency you're evaluating what their own citation share is and how they measure it. The reaction tells you most of what you need to know.
